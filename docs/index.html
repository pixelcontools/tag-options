PIXELCONS Tag Options

Animated guild-tag ideas for geopixels.net. Tell us the number(s) you like. Click any tag to open it and copy its HTML, then paste it into your guild settings → Tag field.

Background:
Tags use only inline SVG animation and the site's own built-in pulse keyframes. No scripts. Source and tag files: github.com/pixelcontools/tag-options (docs/tags/).
